Part Blast

How to play

  • Tap a group. Tap any part that touches at least one part of the same kind. The whole group pops, and new parts keep falling in from the top. The tray never runs out.
  • Go big. A group scores size × size × 5. Pop groups of 4 or more back to back to build a STREAK multiplier up to ×3.
  • Beat the clock. You get 1:00, but you can only tap once per second. Choose every tap wisely.
  • Score. Group points plus a bonus for your biggest group. You can give up and keep what you earned.
